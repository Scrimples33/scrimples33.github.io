After losing both parents, Anne tentatively made contact with her biological family. She went through a host of emotions: her dormant past had become a reality. Anne is now in touch constantly with her new siblings, who have all been wonderfully welcoming, discovering the many similarities between them all. She now has two ‘mes’: the ‘me’ she might have been, and the ‘me’ she is today."
